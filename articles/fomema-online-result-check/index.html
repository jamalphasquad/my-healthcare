      FOMEMA online result check: how to check status, what each result means and what to do next

      There are two official ways to check a FOMEMA result online. Anyone can use the Immigration Department’s free status page with a passport number or name, and employers and agencies can log in to the FOMEMA portal for the full result. Here is how each one works, what the statuses mean, and what to do if a result doesn’t appear.

      Key points
      • Workers can check for free on the Immigration Department’s MyIMMs “Fomema Status Inquiries” page, using their passport number or name and nationality.
      • Employers and agencies log in to the FOMEMA portal at portal.fomema.my and open “Medical Result”. This is also where you print the medical report or unsuitable letter.
      • Under normal circumstances, results reach Immigration headquarters within 7 working days of the examination.
      • A “Pending” status shows which step is holding things up. If nothing appears at Immigration, the usual cause is a mismatch in the passport number, name, gender, date of birth or nationality.
      • A result is valid at Immigration for 180 days. Some Unsuitable results can be appealed, but only if FOMEMA emails to say the case is eligible.

      Where to check a FOMEMA result

      WhereWho can use itWhat you need
      MyIMMs: Fomema Status InquiriesAnyone, including workers. No login needed.Passport number and nationality, or name and nationality
      FOMEMA Online PortalEmployers and agenciesEmployer or agency login

      FOMEMA’s official website is fomema2u.com.my. When we tested older addresses in November 2026, fomema.com.my and fomemaonline.com didn’t work as result checkers.

      Checking a result is free. We found no official route that needs a payment or an agent, so be wary of anyone who charges you just to look up a result.

      How workers can check (MyIMMs)

      1. Open the Fomema Status Inquiries page on the Immigration Department website.
      2. Fill in one of the two forms: Passport No and Citizen (your nationality), or Name and Citizen.
      3. Submit the form to see the status held by Immigration.

      Enter your details exactly as they appear in your passport. This page shows the status held by Immigration. The full medical report and any unsuitable letter are available through the employer’s portal, so ask your employer or agency for a copy.

      Immigration’s systems may change. If the link stops working, check the Immigration Department website.

      How employers and agencies can check (FOMEMA portal)

      1. Log in to the FOMEMA Online Portal as an employer or agency.
      2. Open Medical Result.
      3. Enter the worker code or passport number, then choose Search. You can search up to 10 workers at a time.
      4. For a medical report, go to Registration History and choose Print Report. For an Unsuitable result, use Print Unsuitable Letter.

      What each FOMEMA status means

      StatusWhat it means
      Pending Select ClinicA clinic hasn’t been chosen yet
      Pending ExaminationThe worker hasn’t attended the examination
      Pending Lab and X-ray TransmissionThe laboratory and X-ray results haven’t been sent yet. You may also see Pending Lab or Pending X-ray Transmission.
      Pending for CertificationThe clinic hasn’t sent the result to FOMEMA. Check with the clinic.
      FOMEMA ReviewThe result is under review before release to Immigration
      SuitableThe worker is medically fit, and the employer can go on to renew the permit
      UnsuitableThe worker is not medically fit. The reason appears under “Reason”.
      ExpiredThe result is more than 180 days old, or the form’s validity has ended. A new form must be bought.
      CancelledThe employer has asked for a refund

      How long does a FOMEMA result take?

      • Result to Immigration: within 7 working days of the examination, under normal circumstances.
      • FOMEMA Review: the final result comes within 5 working days after certification.
      • Attending the exam: the registration form is valid for 30 days from payment.
      • Certification: if the examination isn’t certified within 30 days of the clinic visit, the status becomes Expired.
      • Validity: a result is valid at Immigration for 180 days from the examination date.
      • Annual exam: since 16 December 2023, all foreign workers have to be examined every year.

      What to do if a result doesn’t appear

      1. Check the status in the FOMEMA portal. A Pending status tells you which step is holding things up.
      2. If it says Pending for Certification, contact the clinic that did the examination.
      3. If there is no record at Immigration, the most common cause is a mismatch in the passport number, name, gender, date of birth or nationality. Employers can correct this under Register Worker, using the edit option.
      4. If you can’t correct it, email cs@fomema.com.my with the amendment form, passport copies, the permit or approval, and a screenshot. This can take up to 3 working days.

      Contact FOMEMA: WhatsApp 03-2782 8777 or email cs@fomema.com.my, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, except national and Selangor public holidays.

      What happens after the result?

      If the result is Suitable: the employer can go on to renew the work permit (PLKS). Immigration advises applying 3 months before the permit expires.

      If the result is Unsuitable:

      • Appeals are only possible if FOMEMA emails to say the case is eligible. Register the appeal on the portal within 2 weeks of certification, and submit any extra test results within 4 weeks of registering the appeal, or the case will be closed.
      • Not appealable: tuberculosis confirmed by radiologist audit, malaria, pregnancy, HIV and hepatitis B.
      • Syphilis: FOMEMA’s appeal FAQ says it has been appealable since 20 July 2026, with treatment and 6 months’ monitoring, although another FOMEMA page still lists it as non-appealable. Check with FOMEMA.
      • Going home: workers who are not cleared must be sent home. For PLKS holders, the Check Out Memo has been online only since 1 September 2025, through MyPASS@JIM or FWCMS. Immigration counters no longer accept these applications.

      Some long-term conditions, such as diabetes and high blood pressure, can be cleared through a 6-month monitoring programme. See our full guide to the FOMEMA medical check-up for tests, fees and appeals.

      Sabah and Sarawak

      FOMEMA covers Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan. Sabah and Sarawak have their own arrangements. In Sarawak, foreign worker health screening is run through SAFHIS, and employers see a fit or unfit result. In Sabah, check with the state Immigration office or your appointed medical provider.

      Frequently asked questions

      How do I check my FOMEMA result online?

      Open the Immigration Department’s MyIMMs “Fomema Status Inquiries” page and enter your passport number or name with your nationality. Employers can log in to the FOMEMA portal and open “Medical Result”.

      Can a worker check their own FOMEMA result?

      Yes. The MyIMMs page is public and doesn’t need a login. For the full medical report, ask your employer or agency.

      How long does a FOMEMA result take?

      Under normal circumstances, the result reaches Immigration within 7 working days of the examination. Cases under FOMEMA Review get a final result within 5 working days after certification.

      What does Pending for Certification mean?

      The clinic has not yet sent the result to FOMEMA. Contact the clinic that did the examination.

      Why is my FOMEMA result not showing?

      It may still be pending. If there is no record at Immigration, check that the passport number, name, gender, date of birth and nationality match exactly.

      How long is a FOMEMA result valid?

      It is valid at Immigration for 180 days from the examination date.

      Does FOMEMA cover Sabah and Sarawak?

      No. FOMEMA covers Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan. Sabah and Sarawak have their own screening arrangements.

      What is the FOMEMA login page?

      Employers and agencies log in at portal.fomema.my. FOMEMA’s official website is fomema2u.com.my.
