      Pregnancy due date calculator and week-by-week guide for Malaysia

      Use the calculator to find your estimated due date and how many weeks pregnant you are. Then read on for what happens at each stage of pregnancy in Malaysia: when to book your first check-up, which tests and vaccines you’ll be offered, and the warning signs that need a doctor straight away.

      Key points
      • Your due date is usually worked out as 40 weeks (280 days) from the first day of your last period. An early pregnancy scan gives a more accurate date.
      • A pregnancy is full term from 39 weeks. Babies born between 37 and 42 weeks are not considered premature or overdue.
      • Book your first antenatal check-up at a Klinik Kesihatan as soon as you can, ideally before 12 weeks, and bring your pink antenatal book to every visit.
      • Take folic acid (400 mcg a day) from before pregnancy until at least 12 weeks. The whooping cough (Tdap) jab is free at government clinics at 28 to 32 weeks.
      • One-sided tummy pain with bleeding or dizziness in early pregnancy can mean an ectopic pregnancy. Get urgent help.

      Due date calculator

      Enter the first day of your last period and your usual cycle length. The calculator shows your estimated due date, how many weeks pregnant you are, and when key check-ups fall.

      Worked out in your browser: nothing you enter is stored or sent. An early pregnancy scan gives the most accurate due date.

      How your due date is worked out

      • From your last period: the standard method counts 280 days (40 weeks) from the first day of your last menstrual period. This assumes a 28-day cycle, with ovulation around day 14. If your cycles are usually longer or shorter, the calculator shifts the date by the difference.
      • From an early scan: an ultrasound in the first trimester, which measures the baby, is more accurate. If it differs from the date based on your period by more than about a week, doctors usually go by the scan. The Ministry of Health’s perinatal manual says scan dates should be set before 22 weeks.
      • After IVF: the due date is set from the embryo transfer date: 261 days after a 5-day embryo transfer, or 263 days after a 3-day transfer.

      A due date is an estimate. Pregnancy normally lasts between 37 and 42 weeks, and few babies arrive on the exact day.

      Weeks of pregnancyWhat it’s called
      Before 37 weeksPreterm (premature)
      37 weeks to 38 weeks 6 daysEarly term
      39 weeks to 40 weeks 6 daysFull term
      41 weeks to 41 weeks 6 daysLate term

      If your periods are irregular, you stopped the contraceptive pill recently, or you aren’t sure of the date, your doctor will arrange a dating scan.

      Antenatal care in Malaysia

      When and where: register at your nearest Klinik Kesihatan, or with a private doctor, as soon as possible, preferably before 12 weeks. Government clinics give you a pink antenatal record book (Buku Rekod Kesihatan Ibu). Bring it to every appointment, including private ones and when you go into labour.

      How many check-ups: under the Ministry of Health’s Perinatal Care Manual, a healthy first pregnancy has around 10 visits: before 12 weeks, at 16–20, 24–26, 28–30, 32–34 and 35–36 weeks, then weekly from 37 to 40 weeks. Later pregnancies have around 8. You will have more if you have a higher-risk pregnancy.

      Colour codes in the pink book: government clinics tag each pregnancy by risk. White means no risk factors, green is followed up by a clinic doctor, yellow needs a specialist, and red needs immediate referral to hospital.

      Tests at your first visit usually include:

      • Blood pressure, weight and a urine test for protein and sugar
      • Haemoglobin, blood group and Rhesus type
      • Screening for syphilis and HIV, and for hepatitis B where indicated
      • Thalassaemia screening if your red blood cells are small. If you are a carrier, your husband is tested too. See our thalassaemia guide.

      Diabetes in pregnancy: you’ll be offered a glucose tolerance test if you have risk factors, such as a BMI over 27, diabetes in a close relative, a previous baby weighing 4 kg or more, or diabetes in a previous pregnancy.

      Folic acid, iron and vaccines

      • Folic acid: 400 micrograms a day, ideally from 3 months before you conceive until at least 12 weeks of pregnancy, to lower the risk of spina bifida. Women with diabetes or a previous baby with a neural tube defect need a higher 5 mg dose from their doctor.
      • Iron: anaemia is common in pregnancy in Malaysia, and antenatal clinics routinely give iron supplements.
      • Whooping cough (Tdap): free at Ministry of Health clinics since May 2025, given at 28 to 32 weeks. It passes protection to your baby, who is too young to be vaccinated in the first weeks of life.
      • Flu vaccine: recommended in pregnancy by the Ministry of Health. See our flu vaccine guide.

      Early signs of pregnancy and when to test

      Common early signs, usually from 4 to 6 weeks, include a missed period, light spotting, nausea or vomiting, tiredness, sore breasts, needing to pass urine more often, and a metallic taste.

      Home pregnancy tests are most reliable from the first day of a missed period. If you don’t know when your period is due, test at least 21 days after unprotected sex. A positive result is almost certainly correct. A negative result is less reliable, so test again a few days later if your period still hasn’t come.

      Ectopic pregnancy (hamil luar rahim)

      In an ectopic pregnancy, the fertilised egg implants outside the womb, usually in a fallopian tube. It affects about 1 in 90 pregnancies and can’t continue safely. Symptoms usually appear between 4 and 12 weeks:

      • Pain low down in the tummy, often on one side
      • Vaginal bleeding or a brown, watery discharge
      • Pain in the tip of the shoulder
      • Discomfort when passing urine or opening your bowels

      Risk factors include a previous ectopic pregnancy, pelvic infection, tubal surgery, IVF, becoming pregnant with a coil (IUD) in place, smoking and being over 35.

      Call 999 or go to an emergency department

      Sudden, severe tummy pain with dizziness, fainting, feeling sick or looking very pale in early pregnancy can mean an ectopic pregnancy has ruptured. This is an emergency.

      Warning signs later in pregnancy

      Contact your clinic or go to hospital straight away if you have
      • Bleeding from the vagina, or fluid leaking
      • A severe headache, blurred vision or flashing lights
      • Sudden swelling of the face, hands or feet
      • Severe tummy pain, or regular tightenings before 37 weeks
      • A fever of 38°C or more, or severe vomiting
      • Your baby moving less than usual, or fewer than 10 movements in 12 hours from 28 weeks
      • A fit, or sudden breathlessness

      A fever in pregnancy can also be dengue, which needs prompt assessment. See our guide to dengue warning signs.

      Pregnancy in Malaysia: the numbers

      Malaysia recorded 396,175 live births in 2025, 4.5% fewer than in 2024, and the fertility rate fell to a record low of 1.5 children per woman, according to the Department of Statistics. Maternal deaths fell to 20.4 per 100,000 live births in 2025, from 24.3 in 2024.

      Under the Employment Act, working mothers are entitled to 98 days of maternity leave, and married fathers to 7 days of paid paternity leave, subject to conditions.

      Where to start antenatal care

      Your nearest Klinik Kesihatan provides antenatal care and the pink antenatal book. Many private GP clinics can confirm a pregnancy, arrange early blood tests and refer you to an obstetrician. Our directory lists GP clinics across Kuala Lumpur, Selangor and other states.

      Frequently asked questions

      How do I calculate how many weeks pregnant I am? (Kiraan minggu hamil)

      Count the weeks from the first day of your last period. You are counted as pregnant from that date, about two weeks before conception. The calculator above does this for you and adjusts for your cycle length.

      How accurate is a due date calculator?

      It gives an estimate. Pregnancy normally lasts 37 to 42 weeks, and few babies arrive on the exact day. An ultrasound scan in early pregnancy gives a more accurate due date, and your doctor will use it if it differs from your period date.

      When should I go for my first antenatal check-up in Malaysia?

      As soon as possible, preferably before 12 weeks. Register at your nearest Klinik Kesihatan or see a private doctor, and keep your pink antenatal book with you.

      What are the signs of an ectopic pregnancy (hamil luar rahim)?

      Pain low in the tummy, often on one side, vaginal bleeding or brown discharge, shoulder-tip pain, and discomfort when passing urine or opening your bowels, usually between 4 and 12 weeks. Sudden severe pain with dizziness or fainting is an emergency.

      When can I take a pregnancy test?

      Most home tests are reliable from the first day of a missed period. If you aren’t sure when your period is due, test at least 21 days after unprotected sex.

      Is the Tdap vaccine free for pregnant women in Malaysia?

      Yes. Since May 2025, Ministry of Health clinics offer the whooping cough (Tdap) vaccine free to pregnant women at 28 to 32 weeks.
