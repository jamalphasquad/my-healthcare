      Cuti bersalin: maternity and paternity leave in Malaysia, who qualifies and how it’s paid

      Under the Employment Act, women working in the private sector in Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan get at least 98 days of maternity leave (cuti bersalin), and married fathers get 7 days of paid paternity leave. The rules apply whatever your salary, but there are conditions for pay. Here is how it works, plus the different rules for civil servants and in Sabah and Sarawak.

      Key points
      • Private-sector employees get at least 98 consecutive days of maternity leave for each birth, whatever their salary, since 1 January 2023.
      • To be paid, you need at least 90 days’ service in the 9 months before the birth, and you must have worked for that employer in the 4 months before it. Without this, you still get the leave, unpaid.
      • Married fathers get 7 consecutive days of paid paternity leave after 12 months with the same employer, for up to 5 births.
      • Leave can start up to 30 days before the birth. A stillbirth or birth from 22 weeks of pregnancy counts as a confinement, with full maternity and paternity leave.
      • Sabah and Sarawak moved to 98 days of maternity and 7 days of paternity leave from 1 May 2025. Budget 2027 announced up to 98 days per birth for civil servants.

      Maternity leave for private-sector employees

      These rules come from section 37 of the Employment Act 1955, which covers Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan. Since 1 January 2023, they apply to all female employees, whatever their salary, including foreign workers.

      RuleWhat it means
      LengthAt least 98 consecutive days for each birth. Rest days and public holidays count within the 98 days.
      Earliest start30 days before the birth. Days off taken earlier than that don’t count as maternity leave.
      Latest startThe day after the birth
      PayYour ordinary rate of pay, if you meet the conditions below
      Going back earlyPossible with a doctor’s certificate that you are fit, and your employer’s agreement

      Conditions for pay (maternity allowance):

      • at least 90 days of service in the 9 months before the birth, and
      • you must have worked for that employer at some time in the 4 months before the birth.

      If you don’t meet these conditions, you still get the 98 days of leave, but unpaid. The allowance also isn’t payable if you already have 5 or more surviving children at the time of the birth, although you still get the leave. Many employers offer more than the legal minimum, so check your contract.

      If a doctor certifies that you can’t work in late pregnancy, your employer can require you to start leave in the 14 days before the birth.

      Telling your employer

      • Within the 60 days before your due date, tell your employer when your leave will start. You can do this verbally or in writing, but writing is easier to prove.
      • If you don’t give notice, your allowance can be held back until you do. Late notice for a good reason doesn’t cancel it.
      • If you leave a job knowing you will give birth within 4 months, you must tell that employer first, or you lose the allowance from them.

      Paternity leave (cuti paterniti)

      Under section 60FA of the Employment Act, fathers get 7 consecutive days of paid paternity leave for each birth, at their ordinary rate of pay. To qualify, you must:

      • be married to the mother
      • have worked for the same employer for at least 12 months immediately before the leave
      • give notice at least 30 days before the due date, or as soon as possible after the birth

      Paternity leave is limited to 5 births in total.

      Stillbirth, miscarriage and premature birth

      The Employment Act defines a confinement as a birth after at least 22 weeks of pregnancy, whether the baby is born alive or dead. So:

      • a stillbirth from 22 weeks gives the mother full maternity leave and allowance, and the father paternity leave
      • a premature birth from 22 weeks starts maternity leave, which can begin no later than the day after the birth
      • a miscarriage before 22 weeks isn’t covered by maternity leave. Time off is usually taken as sick leave with a medical certificate (MC).

      Losing a baby at any stage is very hard. Ask your doctor or clinic about support, and talk to your employer about the time you need. See our guide to anxiety and mental health support.

      Protection from dismissal

      • While you are pregnant or ill because of your pregnancy, your employer can only dismiss you for wilful breach of contract, misconduct or closure of the business. The employer must prove the dismissal wasn’t because of your pregnancy.
      • Dismissing an employee during maternity leave is an offence, unless the business closes.
      • If a doctor certifies that you are too ill from your pregnancy or the birth to return after your leave, you can’t be dismissed until 90 days after the leave ends.
      • Flexible working: you can apply in writing to change your hours, days or place of work. Your employer must reply in writing within 60 days and give reasons if they refuse.

      Malaysian law doesn’t currently give a right to breastfeeding breaks at work, but many employers allow them. Ask your employer.

      If your employer refuses your rights, you can contact the Labour Department (Jabatan Tenaga Kerja, JTK). The ministry recorded 41 complaints about maternity leave and allowance in 2025.

      Sabah, Sarawak and civil servants

      Sabah and Sarawak: from 1 May 2025, the state Labour Ordinances were amended to give 98 days of maternity leave (up from 60) and 7 days of paternity leave, for all employees regardless of salary. Check the detailed conditions with your employer or the state labour department.

      Civil servants (JPA):

      • The current rule is 60 to 90 days of maternity leave per birth, chosen by the officer, with a lifetime total of 360 days.
      • Budget 2027, tabled on 9 October 2026, announced an increase to a maximum of 98 days per birth and 490 days in total. We haven’t found a JPA circular putting it into effect yet, so check with your HR department.
      • Officers can also take unpaid childcare leave (cuti menjaga anak).
      • Since 1 December 2024, fathers in the civil service can take 1 to 20 days of leave when their wife gives birth, from a shared pool of 20 days a year, up to 5 times in their career.

      Proposed, not yet law: in May 2026 the government announced a post-maternity leave allowance of 80% of salary for up to 30 extra days, funded through the Employment Insurance System. As of October 2026, it was still being studied.

      Health care during and after pregnancy

      • Register for antenatal care at a Klinik Kesihatan or private clinic before 12 weeks if you can. You will get a pink antenatal record book (Buku Rekod Ibu).
      • Antenatal screening includes HIV, hepatitis B, syphilis, diabetes and blood pressure. Folic acid, iron and multivitamins are given free at government clinics, and the whooping cough (Tdap) vaccine has been offered since May 2025.
      • After the birth, nurses carry out home visits, and there is a postnatal check-up about a month later.
      • If you are unwell during pregnancy before your maternity leave starts, a doctor can give you an MC for sick leave.

      See our guides to early pregnancy and your baby’s vaccination schedule.

      Frequently asked questions

      How many days is maternity leave in Malaysia?

      At least 98 consecutive days for each birth under the Employment Act, for private-sector employees in Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan. Sabah and Sarawak also moved to 98 days from 1 May 2025.

      Is maternity leave paid in Malaysia?

      Yes, at your ordinary rate of pay, if you have at least 90 days’ service in the 9 months before the birth and worked for that employer in the 4 months before it.

      How many days is paternity leave in Malaysia?

      7 consecutive days of paid leave for married fathers who have worked for the same employer for 12 months, for up to 5 births.

      When can maternity leave start?

      No earlier than 30 days before the birth and no later than the day after it.

      Can I be dismissed while pregnant?

      Only for wilful breach of contract, misconduct or closure of the business, and your employer must prove it isn’t because of your pregnancy.

      Do I get maternity leave after a stillbirth?

      Yes, if the stillbirth happens at 22 weeks of pregnancy or later. The father also gets paternity leave.

      How long is maternity leave for civil servants?

      Currently 60 to 90 days per birth. Budget 2027 announced up to 98 days per birth, but check with your HR department for when it takes effect.
