      Malay medical terms in English: what cirit birit, bisul, buasir, sawan and other health words mean

      Malaysians often switch between Malay and English when they talk about their health. This guide gives the English meaning of common Malay health words, along with the medical term where it helps. Some words have more than one meaning, so it is always worth describing your symptoms to the doctor too.

      Key points
      • Common words: cirit birit is diarrhoea, muntah is vomiting, loya is nausea, sembelit is constipation and buasir is piles.
      • Skin words: bisul is a boil, kurap is ringworm, panau is a fungal skin patch, ketuat is a wart, kayap is shingles and nanah is pus.
      • Some words have two meanings. Pening can mean dizziness or a headache, lelah can mean asthma or tiredness, and batuk kering can mean a dry cough or tuberculosis.
      • Sawan means a seizure or fit. The old term gila babi for epilepsy is stigmatising, and doctors now use epilepsi.
      • If you aren’t sure of the right word, describe what you feel and when it started. A doctor can work out the rest.

      Talking to your doctor

      You don’t need the medical word. Say what you feel, where it is, when it started, and what makes it better or worse. If a word has two meanings, such as pening or lelah, explain what you mean. If you are more comfortable in another language, ask the clinic if someone can help you, or bring a family member along.

      This guide explains language only and doesn’t diagnose illness. If you are concerned about symptoms, see a doctor. Call 999 for an emergency, and see our guide on clinic or emergency department.

      Frequently asked questions

      What is cirit birit in English?

      Diarrhoea, which means passing loose or watery stools 3 or more times a day.

      What is bisul in English?

      A boil, a painful, pus-filled lump under the skin caused by an infected hair follicle.

      What is buasir in English?

      Piles, also called haemorrhoids: swollen veins in or around the back passage.

      What is sawan in English?

      A seizure or fit. It doesn’t always mean epilepsy.

      What is bengkak in English?

      Swelling or swollen.

      What is selsema in English?

      The common cold. Selsema or selesema can also be used for influenza.

      What is muntah in English?

      Vomiting, or being sick.

      What is nanah in English?

      Pus, the thick fluid that can collect in an infected wound or boil.

      What is kayap in English?

      Shingles, a painful blistering rash caused by the chickenpox virus becoming active again.

      What is the meaning of gila babi?

      It is an old and stigmatising Malay term for epilepsy, and shouldn’t be used. The accepted word is epilepsi.
