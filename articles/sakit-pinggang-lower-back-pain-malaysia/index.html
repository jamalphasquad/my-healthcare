      Sakit pinggang sebelah kiri atau kanan: lower back and side pain, common causes and red flags

      Sakit pinggang means pain around the waist and lower back. Most of the time it is a strained muscle or ligament, and it improves within a few weeks if you keep moving. Pain on one side can sometimes come from a kidney or another organ, so it helps to know the differences and the warning signs.

      Key points
      • Most lower back pain comes from muscles or ligaments, and up to 85% of people recover within 6 weeks. Staying active helps more than bed rest.
      • Muscle pain usually gets worse when you bend, twist or press on the area. Kidney pain is deeper, higher under the ribs, doesn’t change with movement, and often comes with fever or urine symptoms.
      • One-sided causes include kidney stones, kidney infection, the sacroiliac joint, shingles before the rash, and in women, period pain and endometriosis.
      • Paracetamol on its own isn’t recommended for lower back pain. Short courses of anti-inflammatory painkillers can help, but avoid them in suspected dengue.
      • Get emergency help for numbness around the genitals or bottom, bladder or bowel problems, weakness or numbness in both legs, or sudden severe pain with collapse.

      The most common cause: muscle and ligament strain

      Most lower back pain is “non-specific”: it comes from the muscles, ligaments and joints of the back, often after lifting, bending, twisting or sitting for long periods. It is common, and becomes more so with age.

      Signs that it is muscular:

      • a dull ache or spasm over the lower back, on one or both sides
      • worse when you bend, twist, lift, cough, sneeze or press on the area
      • eases when you rest or change position

      In Malaysian studies, back pain was especially common among nurses, drivers and office workers. Heavy lifting, long hours of sitting or standing, awkward postures, obesity, smoking and stress all increase the risk.

      Women: period pain and endometriosis can cause lower back pain. Pelvic girdle pain affects up to 1 in 5 pregnant women, on one or both sides. Ovarian cysts, pelvic infection and ectopic pregnancy can also refer pain to the back. Sudden severe pelvic pain needs urgent care, and if you could be pregnant, take a pregnancy test.

      Is it my kidneys or my back?

      • Kidney pain is felt higher up, under the ribs on one or both sides of the spine, and can spread to the groin. It is a deep, constant ache or sharp pain in waves. It usually doesn’t change when you move, and often comes with fever, nausea, painful urination or blood in the urine.
      • Back pain sits lower, over the spine and the muscles beside it, and changes with movement and position.

      Long-term kidney disease is usually painless, so pain alone isn’t a good guide to kidney health. If you are worried about your kidneys, a urine test and blood test are what count. See our guide to kidney disease.

      What helps muscular back pain

      • Keep moving. Carry on with normal activities as much as you can. If you need to rest, keep it to a day or two, as longer bed rest slows recovery.
      • Heat or ice: heat for stiffness or spasm, ice for a recent strain.
      • Gentle exercise: walking, swimming, stretching and yoga can help, and so can physiotherapy for pain that lasts.
      • Painkillers: UK guidance doesn’t recommend paracetamol on its own for lower back pain. A short course of an anti-inflammatory painkiller such as ibuprofen at the lowest effective dose may help, but it isn’t suitable for everyone, including people with stomach ulcers, kidney disease or heart problems. Avoid anti-inflammatories if you might have dengue. See our painkiller guide.
      • Back belts and corsets aren’t recommended.
      • Massage (urut) may give short-term relief. Choose a practitioner registered with the Traditional and Complementary Medicine Council, and avoid forceful manipulation, especially if you have numbness or weakness.

      Scans aren’t usually needed for ordinary back pain. Doctors consider an MRI if the pain is disabling or lasts more than 6 weeks, after a serious injury, or if there are nerve problems or signs of infection or cancer.

      When to get help

      Call 999 or go to an emergency department for back pain with:

      • numbness around the genitals or bottom
      • new problems controlling your bladder or bowels
      • weakness, numbness or tingling in both legs
      • chest pain
      • pain after a serious fall or accident
      • sudden, severe tummy or back pain with pale skin, breathlessness or collapse, especially in older men or smokers, as this can be a burst aortic aneurysm

      See a doctor urgently for:

      • back or side pain with fever or shivering
      • severe pain that comes in waves, or blood in the urine
      • sudden severe pain, or pain in pregnancy

      See a doctor soon if: you have unexplained weight loss, a history of cancer, a weak immune system, pain that is worse at night or not eased by rest, or pain that isn’t improving after a few weeks.

      Find a clinic for back pain

      A GP can examine your back, test your urine to rule out a kidney problem, prescribe pain relief and refer you for physiotherapy or a scan if needed. Our directory lists clinics across Malaysia, including those open late and at weekends.

      Frequently asked questions

      What is sakit pinggang in English?

      Lower back pain, or pain around the waist. Pain on one side is sometimes called flank pain.

      What causes sakit pinggang sebelah kiri?

      Usually a muscle strain. Other causes include a kidney stone or infection, the sacroiliac joint, shingles, and less often the pancreas or spleen. In women, period pain and endometriosis can cause it.

      What causes sakit pinggang sebelah kanan?

      Usually a muscle strain. A kidney stone or infection, gallbladder problems and, in women, gynaecological causes are also possible.

      How do I know if back pain is from my kidneys?

      Kidney pain is higher, under the ribs, doesn’t change with movement, and often comes with fever, urine symptoms or blood in the urine. Muscle pain changes as you move.

      Should I rest in bed with back pain?

      No more than a day or two. Staying active helps you recover faster.

      When is back pain an emergency?

      With numbness around the genitals or bottom, bladder or bowel problems, weakness in both legs, chest pain, or sudden severe pain with collapse. Call 999.

      Is urut good for back pain?

      Massage may give short-term relief. Use a registered practitioner and avoid forceful manipulation, especially if you have numbness or weakness.
