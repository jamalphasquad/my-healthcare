      About this clinic

      Careclinics Klinik Wong Singh (Country Homes) is part of CareClinics, a Malaysian primary care network run by Careclinics Healthcare Services Sdn Bhd. Alongside walk-in GP care, it lists health screening packages, vaccinations, Pap smears, blood tests and workplace health services.

      Panels and insurance

      The clinic lists these panels and insurers: PMCare, Health Connect, Compumed, AIA, Etiqa Family Takaful, MiCare, HealthMetrics, Red Alert, IA International Assistance, PeKa B40 (ProtectHealth), PERKESO (HSP), FOMEMA, FOMEMA (X-ray). Panel arrangements change, so bring your panel card and check with the clinic before your visit.
