      Frequently asked questions

      Which clinic stays open latest?

      One Medic Bukit Beruang, Careclinics Poliklinik Cahaya (Pandamaran), Careclinics Klinik Seri Indah (Selayang Jaya), Klinik PMC Silibin, Careclinics Poliklinik dan Surgeri Gul, Careclinics Tangga Batu, Careclinics Klinik Wong Singh (Country Homes), Poliklinik Cahaya (Desa Pandan), Klinik Perubatan The Pearl (King Street) and Menara Medical Clinic @ KLIA Terminal 2 stay open until midnight on some days. Closing times vary by day, so check the table above.

      What should I do if a clinic is already closed?

      For anything serious, such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke or heavy bleeding, call 999 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department. Our guide on clinic or emergency department can help you decide.
