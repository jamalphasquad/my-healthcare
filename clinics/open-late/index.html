      Which clinic stays open latest?

      What should I do if a clinic is already closed?

      For anything serious, such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke or heavy bleeding, call 999 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department. Our guide on clinic or emergency department can help you decide.
