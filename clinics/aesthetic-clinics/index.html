      Choosing an aesthetic clinic

      • Check the doctor. Doctors in Malaysia must be registered with the Malaysian Medical Council. You can look them up on the MeRITS register.
      • Start with a consultation. A good clinic assesses you first and explains the risks, recovery time and expected results before any treatment.
      • Ask for a written quote. Prices depend on the treatment, the product used and the number of sessions.

      Frequently asked questions

      Do I need a consultation before an aesthetic treatment?

      Yes. A doctor should assess your skin, medical history and medicines before recommending a treatment. Most clinics book a consultation first.
