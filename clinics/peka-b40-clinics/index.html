      About PeKa B40

      PeKa B40 is the Ministry of Health scheme, run by ProtectHealth, that gives STR recipients and their spouses aged 40 and above a free health screening, with blood tests for diabetes, cholesterol and kidney function. It is being renamed PeKa Sihat. Check your eligibility at kelayakan.pekab40.com.my, and see the official provider search for every panel clinic. Read our PeKa B40 guide for the full list of benefits.

      Frequently asked questions

      Who can get a free screening at a PeKa B40 clinic?

      Malaysians aged 40 and above who receive Sumbangan Tunai Rahmah (STR), and their registered spouses aged 40 and above. There is no need to register first; bring your MyKad.

      Do I need an appointment for PeKa B40 screening?

      Calling ahead is a good idea, as clinics may set times for screening. The screening takes two visits: one for the check-up and tests, and one to go through your results.
