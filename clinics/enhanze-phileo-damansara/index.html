      About this clinic

      Enhanze is run by Aesthetic Medical Services Sdn Bhd and has offered medical aesthetic treatments since 2001. Its Medical Director is Dato’ Dr N. Sutina Nordin, a Universiti Malaya medical graduate. The clinic focuses on non-surgical treatments for skin, ageing, hair and body shape, and also offers regenerative and hormone therapy.

      Enhanze also runs Luminous Clinic at Pavilion Kuala Lumpur.

      Before you book

      • Most treatments start with a consultation with a doctor, who will check whether a treatment is suitable for you.
      • Bring a list of any medicines or supplements you take, and mention allergies or recent procedures.
      • Prices depend on the treatment and the number of sessions, so ask for a written quote.
