      Frequently asked questions

      Which clinics are in Desa Sri Hartamas?

      Klinik Segara Desa Sri Hartamas, 10 Jalan 27/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas, 50480 Kuala Lumpur; Klinik Mediviron Sri Hartamas, 10, Jalan 25/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas, 50480 Kuala Lumpur; Klinik Tanah Air 24 Jam, Plaza Prismaville 1, Jalan 19/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas, 50480 Kuala Lumpur; Poliklinik Hartamas, 44-G, Jalan 27/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas, 50480 Kuala Lumpur; Hartamas Medical Clinic, C-0-13, Plaza Damas 3, Jalan Sri Hartamas 1, Taman Sri Hartamas, 50480 Kuala Lumpur; Klinik Alfie Hartamas, 5-GF, Plaza Prismaville, Jalan 19/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas, 50480 Kuala Lumpur; Pearl's Clinic of Care, A-0-5, Plaza Damas 1, Jalan Sri Hartamas 1, Taman Sri Hartamas, 50480 Kuala Lumpur.

      Is there a clinic open on weekends in or near Desa Sri Hartamas?

      Klinik Segara Desa Sri Hartamas (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Mediviron Sri Hartamas (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Tanah Air 24 Jam (Saturday and Sunday), Cotovia Clinic (Plaza Mont Kiara) (Saturday and Sunday), Hibari Clinic Mont Kiara (Saturday and Sunday) and Nozomi Clinic (Arcoris) (Saturday and Sunday) have weekend hours.

      Which clinic near Desa Sri Hartamas is open latest?

      Klinik Mediviron Sri Hartamas is open until 10pm on some days. Check the clinic page for each day’s hours.

      Is there a 24-hour clinic in or near Desa Sri Hartamas?

      Klinik Tanah Air 24 Jam is open 24 hours. For emergencies, Hospital Kuala Lumpur has a 24-hour emergency department.

      Is there a government clinic in Desa Sri Hartamas?

      Not in Desa Sri Hartamas itself. Nearby, Klinik Komuniti TTDI opens daily from 9am to 6pm, and Klinik Kesihatan Segambut opens on weekdays from 8am to 5pm.
