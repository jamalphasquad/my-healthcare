      Klinik Kesihatan clinics

      Klinik Kesihatan are government health clinics run by the Ministry of Health (KKM). Malaysians pay RM1 to see a doctor and RM5 to see a specialist, and people aged 60 and over are treated free as outpatients. Most open on weekdays, and some have extended evening or weekend hours. Read our Klinik Kesihatan guide for fees, services and what to bring.
