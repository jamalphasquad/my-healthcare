      About this clinic

      Poliklinik Publik Dr Thomas is part of CareClinics, a Malaysian primary care network run by Careclinics Healthcare Services Sdn Bhd. Alongside walk-in GP care, it lists health screening packages, vaccinations, Pap smears, blood tests and workplace health services.

      Panels and insurance

      The clinic lists these panels and insurers: Health Connect, MiCare, HealthMetrics, FOMEMA, FOMEMA (X-ray), Medilink Global, ASP Medical Clinic. Panel arrangements change, so bring your panel card and check with the clinic before your visit.

      Frequently asked questions

      What are the opening hours of Poliklinik Publik Dr Thomas?

      Poliklinik Publik Dr Thomas is open Monday to Saturday, with hours varying by day (latest closing 8pm). See the full weekly hours on this page, and call ahead on public holidays.

      Which panels does Poliklinik Publik Dr Thomas accept?

      The clinic lists Health Connect, MiCare, HealthMetrics, FOMEMA, FOMEMA (X-ray), Medilink Global, ASP Medical Clinic. Check with the clinic before your visit, as panels can change.

      Does Poliklinik Publik Dr Thomas offer vaccinations?

      Yes. The clinic lists vaccinations including influenza, dengue, pneumococcal, meningococcal, typhoid and RSV. Call ahead to check stock.
