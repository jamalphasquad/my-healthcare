      Frequently asked questions

      Can I walk in to a 24-hour clinic at night?

      Yes, 24-hour GP clinics accept walk-ins at any time. Calling first can save a wasted trip, as a doctor may occasionally be away on a home visit or the clinic may be busy.

      Is a 24-hour clinic the same as an emergency department?

      No. A 24-hour clinic can treat everyday illness and minor injuries. For chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke, heavy bleeding or a serious injury, call 999 or go to a hospital emergency department. See our guide on clinic or emergency department.
