      Is there a clinic open on weekends in or near Ampang?

      Klinik Segara Ampang (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Kesihatan Ampang (Saturday and Sunday), Poliklinik Dr. Medic (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Aurora Ampang (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Safa Ampang (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Iltizam Ampang Waterfront (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik As-Salam Ampang (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Hayat 24 Jam Taman Kosas (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Mediviron Ampang Point (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Mediviron Taman Cahaya Indah (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Mediviron Taman Nirwana (Saturday), Poliklinik Cahaya (Desa Pandan) (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Segara The Intermark (Saturday) and Luminous Clinic, Pavilion Kuala Lumpur (Saturday) have weekend hours.

      Which clinic near Ampang is open latest?

      Poliklinik Dr. Medic is open until 11pm on some days. Check the clinic page for each day’s hours.

      Is there a 24-hour clinic in or near Ampang?

      Klinik Aurora Ampang, Klinik Hayat 24 Jam Taman Kosas and Poliklinik Cahaya (Desa Pandan) are open 24 hours. For emergencies, Hospital Ampang has a 24-hour emergency department.

      Is Klinik Kesihatan Ampang open at weekends?

      Yes. Its 2026 hours include an outpatient clinic on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays from 9am to 1pm, and on weekday evenings until 9pm. Hours can change, so call 03-4292 3332 first.
