      About this clinic

      Klinik Kwan is part of CareClinics, a Malaysian primary care network run by Careclinics Healthcare Services Sdn Bhd. Alongside walk-in GP care, it lists health screening packages, vaccinations, Pap smears, blood tests and workplace health services.

      Panels and insurance

      The clinic lists these panels and insurers: Health Connect, Compumed, AIA, MiCare, HealthMetrics, FOMEMA, Selcare. Panel arrangements change, so bring your panel card and check with the clinic before your visit.

      Frequently asked questions

      What are the opening hours of Klinik Kwan?

      Klinik Kwan is open every day, with hours varying by day (latest closing 10pm). See the full weekly hours on this page, and call ahead on public holidays.

      Which panels does Klinik Kwan accept?

      The clinic lists Health Connect, Compumed, AIA, MiCare, HealthMetrics, FOMEMA, Selcare. Check with the clinic before your visit, as panels can change.

      Does Klinik Kwan offer vaccinations?

      Yes. The clinic lists vaccinations including influenza, dengue, pneumococcal, meningococcal, typhoid and RSV. Call ahead to check stock.
