      About this clinic

      Careclinics Sai Poliklinik is part of CareClinics, a Malaysian primary care network run by Careclinics Healthcare Services Sdn Bhd. Alongside walk-in GP care, it lists health screening packages, vaccinations, Pap smears, blood tests and workplace health services.

      Panels and insurance

      The clinic lists these panels and insurers: PMCare, Health Connect, Compumed, AIA, Etiqa Family Takaful, Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB), MiCare, HealthMetrics, Red Alert, IA International Assistance, PeKa B40 (ProtectHealth), PERKESO (HSP), FOMEMA, FOMEMA (X-ray). Panel arrangements change, so bring your panel card and check with the clinic before your visit.

      Frequently asked questions

      What are the opening hours of Careclinics Sai Poliklinik?

      Careclinics Sai Poliklinik is open every day, with hours varying by day (latest closing 9pm). See the full weekly hours on this page, and call ahead on public holidays.

      Which panels does Careclinics Sai Poliklinik accept?

      The clinic lists PMCare, Health Connect, Compumed, AIA, Etiqa Family Takaful, Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB), MiCare, HealthMetrics, Red Alert, IA International Assistance, PeKa B40 (ProtectHealth), PERKESO (HSP), FOMEMA, FOMEMA (X-ray). Check with the clinic before your visit, as panels can change.

      Does Careclinics Sai Poliklinik offer vaccinations?

      Yes. The clinic lists vaccinations including influenza, dengue, pneumococcal, meningococcal, typhoid and RSV. Call ahead to check stock.
