      Is there a clinic open on weekends in or near Cyberjaya?

      Klinik Careclinics Cyberjaya (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Mediviron Cyberjaya (Saturday and Sunday), Aidason Clinic (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Puteri Cyberjaya (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Ohana Cyberjaya (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Hazra Medic Cyberjaya (Saturday and Sunday), ORION Clinic Cyberjaya (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Alam Medic Cyberjaya (Saturday) and Klinik Mediviron Puchong 16 Sierra (Saturday and Sunday) have weekend hours.

      Which clinic near Cyberjaya is open latest?

      Klinik Puteri Cyberjaya is open until 10:30pm on some days. Check the clinic page for each day’s hours.

      Is there a 24-hour clinic in or near Cyberjaya?

      None of the clinics we list in or near Cyberjaya are open 24 hours. See our list of 24-hour clinics, and for emergencies go to Hospital Cyberjaya, which has a 24-hour emergency department, or call 999.

      Is there a government hospital in Cyberjaya?

      Yes. Hospital Cyberjaya, on Persiaran Multimedia in Cyber 11, has an emergency and trauma department open 24 hours a day.
