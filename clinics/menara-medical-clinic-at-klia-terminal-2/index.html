      About this clinic

      Menara Medical Clinic @ KLIA Terminal 2 is part of CareClinics, a Malaysian primary care network run by Careclinics Healthcare Services Sdn Bhd. Alongside walk-in GP care, it lists health screening packages, vaccinations, Pap smears, blood tests and workplace health services.

      Panels and insurance

      The clinic lists these panels and insurers: Health Connect, Compumed, AIA, MiCare, HealthMetrics, Eximius Medical Administration, Integrated Health Plans. Panel arrangements change, so bring your panel card and check with the clinic before your visit.
