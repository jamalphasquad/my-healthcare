      About this clinic

      Luminous Clinic is the Pavilion Kuala Lumpur branch of Enhanze (“Luminous by Enhanze Clinic”). It shares Enhanze’s Medical Director, Dato’ Dr N. Sutina Nordin, and several of its doctors. Alongside non-invasive aesthetic and laser treatments, it offers integrative medicine services such as hormone tests and menopause assessments.

      Before you book

      • Most treatments start with a doctor’s consultation to check what is suitable for you.
      • Bring a list of any medicines or supplements you take, and mention allergies or recent procedures.
      • Ask for a written quote, as prices depend on the treatment and number of sessions.
