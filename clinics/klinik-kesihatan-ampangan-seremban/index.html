      Frequently asked questions

      What are Klinik Kesihatan Ampangan’s opening hours?

      Mon–Thu 8am – 1pm, 2pm – 5pm · Fri 8am – 12:15pm, 2:45pm – 5pm · Sat–Sun Closed. Hours may differ on public holidays, so please call ahead.

      Is Klinik Kesihatan Ampangan open on Sunday?

      No, Klinik Kesihatan Ampangan is closed on Sundays. See our list of clinics open on Sunday.

      Where is Klinik Kesihatan Ampangan?
