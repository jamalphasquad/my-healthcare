      Is there a clinic open on weekends in or near Kajang?

      Klinik Careclinics Kajang (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Kesihatan Kajang (Saturday), Klinik Mediviron Jalan Bukit Mewah, Kajang (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Mediviron Kajang, Bandar Bukit Mahkota (Saturday), Klinik Mediviron Kajang Sentral (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Mediviron Mutiara Heights (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Mediviron Taman Putra Kajang (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik Mediviron Sungai Long (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik As-Salam Kajang (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik As-Salam 24 Jam Bandar Seri Putra (Saturday and Sunday), Klinik As-Salam Kajang Utama (Saturday and Sunday) and Qualitas Health Klinik Era Medik – Sungai Chua, Kajang (Sunday) have weekend hours.

      Which clinic near Kajang is open latest?

      Klinik As-Salam Kajang is open until midnight on some days. Check the clinic page for each day’s hours.

      Is there a 24-hour clinic in or near Kajang?

      Klinik As-Salam 24 Jam Bandar Seri Putra is open 24 hours. For emergencies, Hospital Tengku Permaisuri Norashikin (HTPN) has a 24-hour emergency department.

      What is the new name of Hospital Kajang?

      Hospital Kajang is now listed by the Selangor State Health Department as Hospital Tengku Permaisuri Norashikin (HTPN), on Jalan Semenyih. Its emergency department is open 24 hours.

      Is Klinik Kesihatan Kajang open at night?

      Its 2026 hours include an outpatient clinic on weekday evenings until 9pm, and on Saturdays from 9am to 1pm. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.
