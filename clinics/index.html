About the MediPulih Klinik network

MediPulih Klinik is a family GP network run by MediPulih Group Sdn. Bhd. and part of Bloom Healthcare Group. Since 2024 it has grown to more than 20 branches across Selangor, Kuala Lumpur, Putrajaya, Negeri Sembilan and Melaka, focusing on affordable, family-friendly general practice. Network hotline: 03-2022 5771.
