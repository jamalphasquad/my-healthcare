      About the Klinik Segara network

      Klinik Segara is a group of general practice clinics sharing the Klinik Segara name, with branches across Kuala Lumpur and nearby Petaling Jaya, Subang Jaya, Puchong, Ampang and Cheras. Hours vary by branch, and several are office-hours clinics in commercial buildings, so check the branch page before you go.

      About the Enhanze network

      Enhanze is a medical aesthetics clinic run by Aesthetic Medical Services Sdn Bhd since 2001, led by Medical Director Dato’ Dr N. Sutina Nordin. Its main clinic is at Phileo Damansara in Petaling Jaya, and Luminous Clinic is its branch at Pavilion Kuala Lumpur.

      Visit Enhanze

      About the CareClinics network

      CareClinics is a network of family clinics run by Careclinics Healthcare Services Sdn Bhd since 2003, with branches in the Klang Valley, Negeri Sembilan, Melaka, Johor, Perak and Penang. Several branches are open 24 hours. Hours and services vary by branch, so check the branch page or call before you go.
