      How “near me” works

      • Tap “Use my location” and allow location access when your browser asks. The list is re-ordered with the nearest clinic first, and each card shows the straight-line distance.
      • Turn on “Open now” to see only clinics open at this moment, using Malaysia time.
      • Your location stays private. It is used in your browser only to sort the list, and is not sent to us or stored. See our privacy notice.
      • Prefer not to share your location? Search all clinics by area, postcode or service instead.

      Need a clinic in the middle of the night? See our 24-hour clinics. For chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke or heavy bleeding, don’t wait for a clinic: call 999 or go to the nearest emergency department.

      Frequently asked questions

      How do I find the nearest clinic to me?

      Tap “Use my location” on this page and allow location access. The clinics we list are sorted by distance, nearest first, and you can turn on “Open now” to see only clinics that are open.

      Is my location stored?

      No. Your browser shares your position with this page only after you allow it, and it is used on your device to sort the list. It is not sent to us or saved.

      Why is the nearest clinic still far away?

      We list 44 clinics so far, mostly in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, and are adding more. If we don’t list a clinic near you yet, your nearest Klinik Kesihatan or GP may not appear here.

      Should I go to a clinic or an emergency department?

      A clinic can treat everyday illness and minor injuries. For chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke, heavy bleeding or a serious injury, call 999 or go to an emergency department. See our guide on clinic or emergency department.
