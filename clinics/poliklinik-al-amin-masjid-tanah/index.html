      About this clinic

      Poliklinik Al Amin (Masjid Tanah) is part of CareClinics, a Malaysian primary care network run by Careclinics Healthcare Services Sdn Bhd. Alongside walk-in GP care, it lists health screening packages, vaccinations, Pap smears, blood tests and workplace health services.

      Panels and insurance

      The clinic lists these panels and insurers: Health Connect, AIA, Etiqa Family Takaful, MiCare. Panel arrangements change, so bring your panel card and check with the clinic before your visit.

      Frequently asked questions

      What are the opening hours of Poliklinik Al Amin (Masjid Tanah)?

      Poliklinik Al Amin (Masjid Tanah) is open every day, with hours varying by day (latest closing 10pm). See the full weekly hours on this page, and call ahead on public holidays.

      Which panels does Poliklinik Al Amin (Masjid Tanah) accept?

      The clinic lists Health Connect, AIA, Etiqa Family Takaful, MiCare. Check with the clinic before your visit, as panels can change.

      Does Poliklinik Al Amin (Masjid Tanah) offer vaccinations?

      Yes. The clinic lists vaccinations including influenza, dengue, pneumococcal, meningococcal, typhoid and RSV. Call ahead to check stock.
