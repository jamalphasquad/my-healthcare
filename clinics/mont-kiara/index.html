      Clinics in Mont Kiara

      Mont Kiara is a residential and commercial neighbourhood in north-west Kuala Lumpur, known for its condominiums, international schools and expatriate families. We list 1 clinic here, with opening hours, phone numbers and directions.

      Getting there

      There is no rail station in Mont Kiara itself, so most people drive or use e-hailing.
