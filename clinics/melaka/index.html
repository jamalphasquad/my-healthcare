      Which clinics are in Melaka?

      Klinik Careclinics (Bachang), C04, Rumah Pangsa Bachang Permai, Jalan Tun Fatimah, Taman Bachang Utama, 75350 Melaka; One Medic Bukit Beruang, 49, Jalan Bukit Beruang Utama 2, Taman Bukit Beruang Utama, 75450 Melaka.

      Is there a clinic open on weekends in or near Melaka?

      Klinik Careclinics (Bachang) (Saturday and Sunday) and One Medic Bukit Beruang (Saturday and Sunday) have weekend hours.

      Which clinic near Melaka is open latest?

      One Medic Bukit Beruang is open until midnight on some days. Check the clinic page for each day’s hours.
